Welcome to SmartSearch

Find anything in your files by meaning, keywords or name, and browse your pictures by what they show.

The first time, SmartSearch downloads the two AI models it uses to read your files and pictures (about 700 MB). After that they run on this computer, and your files are not sent anywhere, unless you choose a Claude assistant below.

1. Folders to search

One full path per line. SmartSearch only reads these folders and never moves or changes your files.

2. Choose an assistant

The assistant answers questions from your files. You can change this later in Settings.

You can change all of this later in Settings.
